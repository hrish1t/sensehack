About

        
A national flagship, built for builders.

      

      

        
Sense Hack '26 is a 36-hour national flagship hackathon by IEEE-VIT and IEEE ComSoc. Teams from VIT and colleges across India come together at Anna Auditorium, VIT Vellore, to turn ideas into working prototypes.

        
There are seven tracks, four review phases and hands-on hardware throughout, from component requisition to the final pitch.

        

          
When
December 9–10, 2026

          
Where
Anna Auditorium, VIT Vellore

          
Format
36 hours · 7 tracks · 4 review phases

    Requisition desk
    
        
    Need hardware? Requisition it.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    Hardware is a first-class citizen at Sense Hack. Teams request components from the requisition desk at the start of the event, and build and demo them on site.
    
        
    The component list and the request process will be shared with registered teams.
    
      
    
    
    
  
    

  
    
    
    
      
    FAQ
    
      
    Questions, answered.
    
      
      
    
        
    When and where is it?
    December 9–10, 2026 at Anna Auditorium, VIT Vellore. It runs for 36 hours.
    
        
    Who can take part?
    Teams from VIT and from colleges across India. Eligibility details will be shared on the registration page.
    
        
    What are the tracks?
    Smart Healthcare, Smart Cities, Digital Education, Fintech, Green Tech, Cybersecurity and Open Innovation.
    
        
    How big can a team be, and is there a fee?
    Team size and any fees will be announced with the registration details.
    
        
    Can I build hardware?
    Yes. Hardware demos are part of the event, and teams can request components at the requisition desk.
    
        
    How do I register?
    Use the Register button on this page. Registrations are open.
